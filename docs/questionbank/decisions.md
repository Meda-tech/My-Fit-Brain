# Question bank decisions

> Decided by the product owner on 2026-10-15 in place of clinical sign-off. Any change is made as a new
> question-bank version.

These decisions answer the 20 open questions in `docs/tasks/03-rules-mapping.md`. They are applied by the
patches `packages/questionbank/patches/IA-1.2.patch.json` and `MA-1.1.patch.json`, which turn the live
**MFB Intimacy Assessment 1.1** into **1.2** and **MFB Mind Assessment 1.0** into **1.1**. The Excel files
remain the source of IA 1.1 / MA 1.0; from IA 1.2 / MA 1.1 onward the database is the source of truth.

Notes:
- T7 and T8 belong to module G, so D5 and D6 only apply when G is chosen (those questions are only shown then).
- Effective format order in IA 1.2 (flags with `force_format` win first, in flag `sort` order):
  SAFETY → RELATIONSHIP_SAFETY_CHECK → LOW_REPAIR_MOTIVATION (flags) → SOLO_INDIVIDUAL → PARTNER_NOT_WILLING →
  COUPLE_ONLY → INDIVIDUAL_ONLY → MIXED → DEFAULT_INDIVIDUAL.

## Changes made while applying the decisions

| Item | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Protected flags (CRISIS, SAFETY) | Validation now lets a new version **add** to a protected flag's effects (e.g. a `notify` recipient) but never remove or change an effect or the condition. | D19 adds `counsellor` to `notify`; protection is against weakening safety rules |
